Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains how My App ("the app") handles your information.

Information we access

The app uses Google Sign-In. When you sign in, it asks your permission to access files that the app creates or opens in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). The app cannot see your other Drive files.

How we use your information

Your Google account access is used only to save, read, and update the app's files in your own Google Drive. It is not used for advertising or any other purpose.

Data storage and sharing

Your files stay in your own Google Drive. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data or files on our own servers, and we do not share them with third parties.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already saved in your Drive remain yours and can be deleted by you at any time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
